Questions you might have

Is Montraly a real bank?

No. It's a portfolio project that shows how a business banking product could look and feel. It isn't licensed, and it can't hold or move real money.

Is any of the data real?

None of it. Every name, balance and transaction is made up so you can click around freely.

How would Montraly make money?

Through a small fee on transfers. There are no monthly plans, so you only pay when you move money. The demo doesn't charge anything.

Do I need to sign up to try it?

No. Open any screen and use it. The "Open an account" flow walks through business verification, and it only saves to your own browser.

What is it built with?

React 18 and plain CSS, loaded from a CDN, with no build step. Each screen has its own HTML, CSS and JS, and they share one sidebar and one set of colour tokens.

Can I see the code?

Yes. The project lives on GitHub at Figmaboy-bot/Fintech-Web-app.
